## Conclusion (claim wording for the paper)
- A training-free, pixels-only, per-image adaptive vocabulary selector improves a frozen Grounding DINO-T on held-out LAE-80C test images: **AP50 ×5.3 under standard and ×2.7 under source-aware scoring vs the full 80-class prompt**. It is robust by three checks under both protocols.
- It also robustly beats a fixed-size selector with the same encoder.
- The gain is concentrated on source datasets like those the selector's encoder was trained on (DIOR/DOTA: ×3.5). It is absent on fine-grained, unfamiliar label spaces (FAIR1M, xView) (D46).
- Absolute accuracy remains low (oracle 0.107), bounded by the frozen detector.
